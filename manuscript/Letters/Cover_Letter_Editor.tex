Dear Editor,

We thank you and the reviewers for the careful second-round assessment. We
have addressed every comment point by point, and on the Round 2 revision
submission page we have separately submitted a point-to-point cover letter
to each of the three reviewers. No new training experiments were required;
the revisions concern presentation, mathematical justification, wording and
internal consistency.

The substantive changes are summarised as follows:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Figures.} All seven figures were redrawn as publication-ready
vector files, with legends moved outside the plotting areas and enlarged
fonts, markers, line widths and cap sizes. Every data point and reported
number is unchanged.
\item \textbf{Theoretical clarification.} Proposition~1 is now stated in
three layers: the exact case, unconditional distribution-level
total-variation bounds, and a pointwise posterior bound that holds only
under an explicit inter-model likelihood-ratio condition, under which the
bound is $\mathrm{TV}\le\tanh(\alpha)$; the KL corollary is conditioned on
the same assumption.
\item \textbf{Statistical wording.} We consistently distinguish a
non-significant difference (statistically tied / on par), equivalence
certified by a TOST with a stated margin using the correct 90\% confidence
interval, and superiority. We make no superiority claim, and the
effectiveness boundary is characterised empirically rather than as a
guaranteed improvement.
\item \textbf{Editorial cleanup.} The duplicated data-availability
statement was removed, followed by a full consistency check of equations,
tables, cross-references and terminology.
\end{itemize}

We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published
elsewhere, and is not under consideration by any other journal. All authors
have approved the revised version and the author list is unchanged. The
authors declare no conflict of interest. All references are necessary and
are substantively cited within the manuscript, with none included merely
to inflate the bibliography. All data and the reproducible code are
publicly available at
\url{https://github.com/qsongcai/CIFA}.

We hope the revised manuscript now meets the requirements and remain at
your disposal should any further adjustments be needed.
